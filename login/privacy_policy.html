This Privacy Policy describes how the individual developer and operator of the Kemyze mobile application ("we," "us," or "our"), based in the United States, collects, uses, and protects your personal information. By downloading, accessing, or using Kemyze, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We collect only the personal information necessary to provide and operate Kemyze:

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality & Account Management: To create and maintain your account, authenticate your identity, and provide Kemyze’s core features.
  • Optional Email Communications: If you explicitly opt in, we will use your email address to send you updates, announcements, or other communications related to Kemyze. You may opt out or unsubscribe at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.
  • Support & Security: To respond to your inquiries, troubleshoot technical issues, and protect the security and integrity of the app.

3. No Tracking, No Ads, and No Retargeting

We respect your privacy and maintain a strict policy against behavioral tracking and advertising:

  • No Third-Party Tracking: Kemyze does not track your activity across third-party apps or websites.
  • No Advertisements: Kemyze does not display third-party advertisements.
  • No Meta/Facebook Pixel or Retargeting: We do not use the Facebook (Meta) Pixel, advertising SDKs, or any retargeting technologies to profile you or serve targeted ads.
  • Do Not Track (DNT) Signals (CalOPPA Disclosure): Because Kemyze does not engage in cross-site or cross-app behavioral tracking, your user experience and our data practices remain the same regardless of whether your device or browser transmits a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration. We only share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting, database, or email delivery services) that process data solely on our behalf to keep Kemyze running, bound by strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: When required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or enforceable governmental requests, or to protect our rights, safety, and property.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain your personal information only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards (such as encryption in transit and secure authentication) to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

6. Your Privacy Rights

A. California Residents (CalOPPA & CCPA/CPRA)

Under the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the CPRA, California residents have the right to:

  • Know and Access: Request details about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, used, or disclosed about you.
  • Delete: Request the deletion of your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Correct: Request the correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Opt Out of Sale/Sharing: Kemyze does not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Non-Discrimination: Exercise any of your privacy rights without receiving discriminatory treatment.

B. European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Swiss Residents (GDPR)

If you access Kemyze from the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) based on the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing your name/username, email, phone number, and camera input to provide the Kemyze app features you request.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Sending promotional or update emails (only when you actively opt in) and accessing your device camera via system permissions. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Maintaining app security and preventing fraud.

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase ("right to be forgotten"), restrict or object to processing, request data portability, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

International Data Transfers: Kemyze is operated from the United States. If you reside outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

7. Children's Privacy

Kemyze is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it immediately.
