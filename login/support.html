Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable camera access for Kemyze?

Kemyze uses your device's camera for core features. If the camera isn't working, open your device's Settings, scroll down to Kemyze, and make sure Camera permissions are toggled on.

How do I update or delete my account and personal data?

You can request to access, update, or permanently delete your account (including your name/username, email address, and phone number) by emailing mattbrimberry@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

Where can I read Kemyze's Privacy Policy?

You can review how we handle and protect your information at any time on our Privacy Policy page.
